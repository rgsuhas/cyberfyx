# Cyberfyx.net Analysis Report

## 1. Introduction
This document contains a comprehensive analysis of the cyberfyx.net website, covering site structure, page-by-page content, technical stack, and security observations.

## 2. Website Overview
Cyberfyx is an IT and cybersecurity solutions provider focused on empowering organizations with intelligence-led protection, regulatory readiness, and operational resilience.

## 3. Site Structure
The website is structured around the core services offered, including Cybersecurity, IT Security, Endpoint Management, Core Industries, and Trainings. It includes standard corporate pages such as About Us, Leadership Team, Careers, and Contact.

## 4. Subdomains Identified
*No active functional subdomains were resolving during standard enumeration. General subdomains (e.g. blog, portal, careers) either point back to the main domain or do not have DNS records.*

## 8. Observations & Insights
- **Significant Template Leftovers**: A large number of pages and blog posts on the site (`/get-a-quote/`, `/faq/`, `/team-member/`, `/career/`, `/portfolio/`, etc.) contain unedited dummy content from a seemingly Logistics/Transportation WordPress theme.
- **Security & Credibility**: Despite being a cybersecurity organization, the site has several broken links (e.g., in the Industries page pointing to a staging `hotpink-lyrebird...` environment) and active dummy forms/emails which could impact credibility and appear unprofessional.
- **Company History**: References to "KRISH CORP" suggest it is either a parent company or a former name, which might confuse visitors as it appears briefly and unexpectedly on the About page.

## 9. Conclusion
The cyberfyx.net website serves as an informational brochure for their core offerings like Endpoint Management, IT Security services, and ISO certification readiness. While the main service pages provide a comprehensive look into their offerings and the extensive leadership team, there is a significant amount of administrative clean-up required. Specifically, the removal of leftover pages from a generic logistics WordPress theme, updating dummy contact information, and fixing broken staging links. Addressing these presentation issues should be a top priority to ensure the website genuinely reflects the professional standards of a cybersecurity organization.
