 About Cyberfyx 
 											Connect With Us 
 Cyberfyx delivers intelligence-led IT and cybersecurity solutions that empower organizations with protection, compliance, and resilient digital transformation. 
 Get Quote 
 Cyberfyx brings robust capabilities in delivering scalable, end-to-end IT security 
 services tailored for diverse industry needs. Our team comprises certified security 
 professionals with hands-on experience across cloud, on-premise, and hybrid 
 infrastructures. With proven execution in vulnerability management, compliance 
 (ISO 27001, ISO 27701, ISO 22301 etc), endpoint protection, and red team 
 operations, we serve clients ranging from startups to large enterprises. Backed by 
 strong GRC expertise and strategic advisory support , Cyberfyx ensures 
 organizations are both resilient and audit-ready. Our adaptable staffing and 24/7 
 support models ensure rapid response and long-term security program success. 
 ISO 27001:2022 – Information Security Management System 
 Establishes a risk-based security framework to protect information assets. 
 Cyberfyx enables full lifecycle support from policy creation to audit preparation. 
 ISO 27701:2019 
 – 
 Privacy Information Management System 
 Extends ISO 27001 for data privacy governance aligned with global laws like GDPR. 
 We help organizations manage personal data processing with accountability and transparency. 
 ISO 22301:2019 
 – Business Continuity Management System 
 Ensures preparedness and resilience against business disruptions and disasters. 
 Cyberfyx supports BIA, continuity planning, and recovery drills for certification. 
 ISO 20000-1:2018 – IT Service Management System 
 Sets global standards for delivering managed IT services effectively and efficiently. 
 Our experts implement ITSM processes aligned with business performance goals. 
 ISO 27017 &amp; 27018 – Cloud Security &amp; Privacy Controls 
 Provides cloud-specific guidance for security and personal data protection. 
 We help cloud service providers and customers secure and govern cloud environments. 
 ISO 42001 
 – AI Management System 
 A new framework for responsible, secure, and transparent use of Artificial Intelligence. 
 Cyberfyx assists in aligning AI systems with ethical, legal, and risk-based standards. 
 CSA STAR – Cloud Security Alliance Certification 
 Recognized cloud security certification combining ISO 27001 with CSA controls. 
 We guide SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS providers through gap analysis and self-assessments. 
 TISAX 
 – Trusted Information Security Assessment Exchange 
 Required by the automotive industry for secure handling of sensitive data. 
 Cyberfyx helps suppliers prepare for TISAX through risk assessments and audit support. 
 Internal Audits &amp; Assessments 
 Regular reviews to ensure ongoing compliance, effectiveness, and readiness. 
 Cyberfyx delivers intelligence-led IT and cybersecurity solutions that empower organizations with protection, compliance, and resilient digital transformation.
