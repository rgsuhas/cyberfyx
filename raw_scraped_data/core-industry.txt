 About Cyberfyx 
 											Connect With Us 
 Cyberfyx delivers intelligence-led IT and cybersecurity solutions that empower organizations with protection, compliance, and resilient digital transformation. 
 Get Quote 
 Cyberfyx provides end-to-end support—from gap assessments, documentation, 
 training, and internal audits to certification liaison and post-certification 
 maintenance. We ensure that management systems are not just compliant, but 
 also integrated, efficient, and aligned with business performance objectives. 
 ● 
 ISO 9001 – Quality Management System 
 We help organizations establish robust QMS frameworks for consistent product and service 
 delivery. Cyberfyx supports policy development, process mapping, internal audits, and certification 
 readiness. 
 ● 
 ISO 14001 – Environmental Management System 
 Cyberfyx enables companies to implement eco-conscious operational controls, perform 
 environmental impact assessments, and maintain regulatory compliance with sustainability goals. 
 ● 
 ISO 45001 – Occupational Health &amp; Safety Management System 
 We assist in creating safe work environments by implementing hazard identification, risk mitigation, 
 and safety culture practices aligned with ISO 45001 requirements. 
 ● 
 AS9100 – Aerospace Quality Management 
 Tailored for the aerospace sector, Cyberfyx supports the implementation of AS9100 standards with 
 a focus on product safety, risk management, and regulatory alignment. 
 ● 
 IATF 16949 – Automotive Quality Management System 
 For the automotive supply chain, Cyberfyx delivers gap assessments, process optimization, and 
 audit support to help organizations meet the quality expectations of OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers. 
 ● 
 Fire Safety Audit 
 We assess fire protection systems, evacuation protocols, and risk controls as per NBC and local fire 
 regulations. Cyberfyx ensures your premises meet safety compliance and insurance requirements. 
 ● 
 Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) Audit 
 Ensures responsible sourcing and sustainable forest management practices in line with FSC Chain 
 of Custody (CoC) standards. Cyberfyx supports FSC audit readiness, documentation, traceability, 
 and supplier compliance. 
 ● 
 SEDEX Audit (SMETA) 
 Social compliance audit based on labor rights, health &amp; safety, environment, and business ethics. 
 Cyberfyx helps you prepare for SMETA 2/4-pillar audits and improve your supplier credibility. 
 ● 
 Awareness &amp; Internal Auditor Training 
 We deliver structured training for ISO standards, compliance frameworks, and audit skills. 
 Our programs equip teams to understand requirements and independently conduct internal audits. 
 Cyberfyx delivers intelligence-led IT and cybersecurity solutions that empower organizations with protection, compliance, and resilient digital transformation.
